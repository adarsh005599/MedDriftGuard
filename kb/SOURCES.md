# Approved Medical Knowledge Base Sources

All knowledge indexed by MedDriftGuard must originate exclusively from verified, authoritative, and licensed public-health sources.

## Source Registry

| Source ID | Source Name | Base URL | License / Terms | Medical Scope | Ingestion Status |
|-----------|-------------|----------|-----------------|---------------|------------------|
| `medlineplus` | MedlinePlus (US National Library of Medicine) | https://medlineplus.gov/healthtopics.html | Public Domain (U.S. Government work) | General health conditions, symptoms, prevention | Approved |
| `who` | World Health Organization (WHO) Fact Sheets | https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets | CC BY-NC-SA 3.0 IGO (With attribution) | Global disease awareness, infectious diseases | Approved |
| `mohfw` | Ministry of Health and Family Welfare (MoHFW, India) | https://mohfw.gov.in | Government Open Data License - India (GODL) | India health programs, seasonal alerts | Approved |
| `icmr_ncdc` | ICMR & NCDC Guidelines | https://www.icmr.gov.in , https://ncdc.mohfw.gov.in | Open Access / Public Health Information | Endemic diseases (Dengue, Malaria, Typhoid) | Approved |

## Strict Exclusions (Do Not Ingest)
1. **No Drug Dosing Tables**: Absolute prohibition on pharmaceutical dosage charts, titration guidelines, or prescription calculators.
2. **No Commercial or Unverified Blogs**: Health blogs, user forums (e.g. Reddit, Quora), and commercial pharmaceutical advertisements must never be ingested.
3. **No Unlicensed Sources**: Any source lacking an explicit public domain or open-access license is skipped automatically during ingestion.
